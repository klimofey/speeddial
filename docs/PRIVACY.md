# Dialy — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026‑05‑25_

Dialy ("the extension") replaces Chrome's New Tab page with a customizable dashboard.
It is designed to be private by default.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Dialy has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. The developer does
not collect, receive, store, sell, or share any of your data.

## Where your data lives

Everything you create — tiles, layout, widget settings, notes, themes, and cached
icons/images — is stored **locally in your browser** through Chrome's extension
storage. If you have Chrome Sync enabled, Chrome may sync it to your own Google
account; the developer never has access to it.

## Permissions and why they're used

- **storage / unlimitedStorage** — save your dashboard and cached images locally.
- **favicon** — show each site's icon using Chrome's built‑in local favicon service.
- **history** — read your local browsing history to populate the optional "Recent
  sites" row. It is read on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.
- **scripting** + host access — only when you click "Find a better image": Dialy opens
  the site you are adding in a background tab and reads its logo to suggest a tile icon.
- **bookmarks** (optional) — the Bookmarks widget lists a bookmark folder you choose.
  Requested only when you add that widget.
- **tabGroups + tabs** (optional) — the Tab Groups widget lists your currently open tab
  groups and their tabs. Requested only when you add that widget.

Optional permissions are requested only at the moment you first use the related feature.

## Third‑party services (only when you turn a feature on)

Some features send data **directly from your browser to a third party** to work. Dialy
has no servers of its own, so nothing is routed through us:

- **Search suggestions** (optional): the text you type in the search box is sent to your
  chosen provider (DuckDuckGo or Google) to return suggestions. Can be turned off in
  Settings.
- **Cloud translation** (optional, off by default): if on‑device translation is
  unavailable and you enable cloud fallback, the text you translate is sent to MyMemory
  (api.mymemory.translated.net).
- **Icon lookup**: when finding a tile icon, the address of the site you are adding may
  be sent to favicon services (Google, DuckDuckGo, icon.horse) or fetched directly to
  read its logo.

These requests are subject to the respective third parties' own privacy policies.

## Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or share your data, and we cannot — it never reaches us.
